    Your money stays in your PayPal account until it’s spent.
    
      
    Approving a plan places an authorization hold, not a transfer. Each purchase captures only its own price from that hold. When the week closes, the rest is released to you.
    

      
      
    PayPal won’t void a hold after a partial capture (PREVIOUSLY_CAPTURED), so Remit lets the leftover lapse when the authorization expires and says so in your ledger. A hold with nothing captured is voided right away.
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    The money rules live in code. No prompt can change them.
    
      
    The agent decides what to buy. The wallet decides whether it can. Everything below is enforced on the server, outside the model.
    
      
    
        
    Only you approve money
    A budget or top-up opens only after you approve it on PayPal. The agent can ask; it can’t say yes.
    
        
    Lines are envelopes
    A purchase can use its own line, then the shared buffer. Never another line.
    
        
    No overspending
    Remit checks the line and the total first, and PayPal refuses any capture larger than the hold.
    MAX_CAPTURE_AMOUNT_EXCEEDED
    
        
    An independent purpose check
    A separate Claude call sees the line and the purchase, but not the page the agent read. If it can’t be reached, Remit refuses.
    
        
    Refusals are recorded
    Every refused purchase is in your ledger with its reason. The agent is told to ask you, not to work around it.
    
        
    One purchase at a time
    Spends are serialized, so two purchases at once can’t both pass the same check.
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Give any agent a wallet.
    
        
    Remit’s MCP server works with Claude Desktop, Cursor or your own agent. It holds no PayPal keys. Every call goes to your wallet, which applies the same rules.
    
        
    
          
    get_balance
    Each line with what’s left, the total still held, and anything waiting for you.
    
          
    request_budget
    Propose a plan of up to 28 days, with a reason for every line.
    
          
    request_topup
    Ask you for more money. It’s a new approval on PayPal.
    
          
    spend
    Pay for one purchase from one line, after the checks.
