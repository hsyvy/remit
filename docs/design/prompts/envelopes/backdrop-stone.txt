A wide editorial still-life backdrop for a luxury private bank's website, photographed with a medium-format camera.
A honed warm-white limestone (travertine-like, very fine, almost no holes) tabletop seen from slightly above, its far edge meeting a smooth warm plaster wall. Soft north daylight from the upper left, one long, very soft architectural shadow falling diagonally across the wall and stone, subtle and elegant. The stone fills the lower 55% of the frame and is completely empty and clean, ready for objects to be placed on it.
Quiet, expensive, calm, restrained. Palette of warm ivory, oat and pale stone (around #EFEBE4, #E4DED4, #D9D2C6). Very low contrast, gentle grain, no objects, no plants, no people, no text, no logos.
