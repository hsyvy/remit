IDENTITY "Precision":

- Wordmark: "remit" in lowercase, a precise neo-grotesk sans in medium weight, tight but comfortable spacing, very clean.

- Typography: one neo-grotesk family in two weights, with tabular figures; a monospace only for IDs.
Every piece of text must be crisp, correctly spelled exactly as given, and in English. Keep printed text minimal and elegant. No gradients, no glow, no neon, no sparkles, no robots, no AI imagery, no clutter, no props like plants, coffee or pens.
