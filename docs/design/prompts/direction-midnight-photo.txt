IDENTITY "Midnight":
- Monogram: a refined serif capital R set inside a thin square frame; the R's diagonal leg reaches down and stops exactly on the bottom edge of the frame without crossing it, meaning "within its remit". Thin, precise, elegant.
- Wordmark: "Remit" in a refined high-contrast transitional serif, regular weight, sentence case, slightly generous letter spacing.
- Colors: deep midnight navy #0E1A2B, bone white #EFEBE3, champagne #C8B68E used only for foil and fine details, slate gray #5B6675. Money states are muted: held in soft amber #B8893A, refused in muted brick #A5483D.
- Typography: the serif for headings and large figures; a neutral, very legible sans for interface text.
Every piece of text must be crisp, correctly spelled exactly as given, and in English. Keep printed text minimal and elegant. No gradients, no glow, no neon, no sparkles, no robots, no AI imagery, no clutter, no props like plants, coffee or pens.
