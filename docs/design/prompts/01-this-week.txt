Top bar: wordmark "Remit" on the left; tabs "This week", "History", "Agents", "Settings"; on the right small pill tags "PayPal sandbox" and "Claude · active".

Directly under the top bar, a full-width bordered strip titled "Needs you (1)": "Top-up request · $104.50 for Sam's birthday gift · 'You picked the headphones, $95.00 plus about 10% for shipping.'" with buttons "Review" and "Decline".

Annotations: 1 "Needs you" holds only decisions that move money, 2 the remit bar: spent / held / released, 3 each line is its own envelope, 4 refused purchases stay in the ledger with the reason, 5 the agent panel is secondary to the money.
STYLE: a clean low-fidelity UX wireframe, the kind a senior product designer draws in Figma before visual design. Pure white background, grayscale only (#222 lines and text, #888 secondary text, #e5e5e5 placeholder fills), 1px hairline strokes, square-ish corners with 6px radius, a plain sans-serif for all text, monospace for every money amount. No colors, no gradients, no shadows, no glow, no 3D, no illustrations, no icons except simple outline glyphs, no device mockup, no hands, no perspective: a flat, straight-on screen. All text must be legible, spelled exactly as given, in English. Add 4 to 6 small numbered annotation circles (black circle, white number) placed next to key elements, with a narrow annotation column on the right edge listing what each number means in one short line.
