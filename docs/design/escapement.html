Your money stays in your PayPal account until it’s spent.

      
Approving a plan places an authorization hold, not a transfer. Each purchase captures only its own price. When the week closes, the rest is released to you.

      
      
PayPal won’t void a hold after a partial capture (PREVIOUSLY_CAPTURED), so Remit lets the leftover lapse when the authorization expires and says so in your ledger.

    

  


  

    

      
The rules live in code. No prompt can change them.

      
The agent decides what to buy. The wallet decides whether it can.

      

        
Only you approve money
A budget or top-up opens only after you approve it on PayPal. The agent can ask; it can’t say yes.

        
Lines stay separate
A purchase can use its own line, then the shared buffer. Never another line.

        
No overspending
Remit checks first, and PayPal refuses any capture larger than the hold.
MAX_CAPTURE_AMOUNT_EXCEEDED

        
An independent purpose check
A separate Claude call sees the line and the purchase, not the page the agent read. If it can’t be reached, Remit refuses.

        
Refusals are recorded
Every refused purchase is in your ledger with its reason.

        
One purchase at a time
Spends run one after another, so two can’t pass the same check at once.

      

    

  


  

    

      

        
Give any agent a wallet.

        
Remit’s MCP server works with Claude Desktop, Cursor or your own agent. It holds no PayPal keys. Every call goes to your wallet, which applies the same rules.

        

          
get_balance
Each line with what’s left, and anything waiting for you.

          
request_budget
Propose a plan of up to 28 days, with a reason for every line.

          
request_topup
Ask you for more money. It’s a new approval on PayPal.

          
spend
Pay for one purchase from one line, after the checks.
