Set up a wallet your agent can’t overspend.

Three steps. Nothing is held or spent until you approve a plan, and you can disconnect at any time.

  1. 1

    Connect PayPal

    Remit places holds on your PayPal account when you approve a plan. It never sees your card or bank details.

  2. 2

    Connect your agent

    Waiting for the agent

    Add Remit to the agent you already use. It gets four tools and a token, and no access to PayPal.

    "remit": {
  "command": "npx",
  "args": ["remit-mcp"],
  "env": { "REMIT_AGENT_TOKEN": "remit_agt_7Qx…3f9c" }
}

    Remit moves on as soon as the agent calls get_balance.

  3. 3

    Ask for a first plan

    Your agent looks at what you usually buy and proposes a budget with a reason for every line. You review it, change it, and approve it on PayPal.
